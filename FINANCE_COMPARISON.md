## A common research protocol (ours, not the accounts' advice)

1. Freeze the public timestamp, exact text and instrument before comparing outcomes; never update a historical entry/stop silently.
2. Record market venue, account equity, collateral currency, notional, gross/net delta, borrow/funding costs, slippage and liquidation path separately.
3. Separate planned scaling from loss-driven doubling; require an ex-ante aggregate loss boundary rather than retroactive “I had a plan.”
4. Track negative cases and absent follow-ups alongside successes. A source-linked case study is not a statistical sample of all trades.
5. Compare returns over identical windows against a specified benchmark, net of costs, with deposits/withdrawals and drawdown. No account in this archive supplies enough verified data for such a cross-account performance table.
6. Before calling something “arb,” model redemption/withdrawal freezes, rate changes, depegs, venue failure and access loss. Cognitive/valuation “套利” can retain full directional risk.

**Conclusion:** the useful shared idea is explicit decision decomposition and failure-mode accounting. Their methods, horizons and risks are not interchangeable. No audited profits, promised returns, private identities or live financial actions are supplied by this package.
