It is a **narrative-rotation operator**: treat the market as a sequence of stories (NFTs, GameFi, “new stories,” Friend.tech, memecoins, later US midterms / AI-stock), try to arrive early with an information advantage, size into strength, and leave the last cycle’s bag alone unless you have a cost advantage.

**What this model is not.** It is not evidence of a verified edge. It is not a complete risk manual. Formal invalidation language is rare. Self-reported P&L is unverified. Later posts show him violating his own stated discipline. Monetization (calls, groups, exchange links) sits next to the advice.

## Decision frameworks, grounded in posts

### Signal vs noise

**Explicit / close-to-explicit.** On 15 Jul 2022 he explained why he had stopped listening to Twitter Spaces: work, repeating low-quality takes (“大家在透支自己的认知”), and:

> 第三点就是熊市越操作越容易亏损，过去的叙事肯定会有巨大变化，不如静心学习

Source: [1547633896166412288](https://x.com/silverfang88/status/1547633896166412288) (2022-07-15 01:27:29 GMT+8).

On 16 Jun 2023, after “研究参与了超级多早期项目,” he wrote that most things looked already picked over and “新的故事确实难找”: [1669709142632857601](https://x.com/silverfang88/status/1669709142632857601).

The Aug 2023 rule list (above) elevates **information gathering** over opinion consumption.

**Interpretation.** Noise, in his usage, is recycled narrative and compulsive trading in a market that has already used up its last story. Signal is primary-source collection (sites, accounts, docs, on-chain tools) plus noticing when a *new* story is actually new. This is not a quantitative filter.

**Practical test (ours).** For one week, log every trading input as “primary source,” “recycled take,” or “social proof.” Compare your activity on high-noise days with the 2022 advice to trade less in a bear. The test is about *your* process, not a claim that following him would have been profitable.

## Contradictions, failure modes, monetization, survivorship

1. **“Trade less / study” vs frequent calling and 梭.** 2022 bear advice and 2023–2026 high-activity calling/timing posts can both be genuine and still be in tension. A reader who only keeps one side will misread him.
2. **Written scale-out / 止损 vs 满仓挨打.** The useful document is the pair, not either post alone.
3. **Bubble + 梭.** The 2026 pin holds “历史级别泡沫” and “低位梭进去” together. That is a timing bet inside a bubble call, not a conservative risk stance.
4. **Self-reported performance.** The pin’s “本金300多万美金 … 三个月最高赚了360万美金ibkr账号” is a claim, with other accounts explicitly withheld. There is no independent brokerage verification in this research, and no backtest. **Do not treat it as proof of edge.**
5. **Survivorship and selection.** “那就不展示了” is an open selection filter. Public year-end or pin highlights over-represent paths that can be shown.
6. **Monetization and conflicts.** Bio links (Telegram, OKX), Friend.tech key-calling with prices and “大群,” memecoin bot shout-outs, and midterm-content positioning all create an incentive to be early, loud, and followed. That does not make every idea false; it does mean performance theater and distribution are part of the genre.
7. **Coverage failure mode for the reader.** If you build a 2025–2026 theory from 25 + 203 posts, you are mostly reading samples, not the years.

## What a careful reader can actually take

The transferable *study* object is: **separate the next story from the last bag; treat information collection as work; separate a long thesis from the entry; write down exits; then audit yourself when you don’t follow them.** None of that is a promise that copying his 2023 memecoin ladder or his 2026 midterm 梭 is intelligent. Those are dated, market-specific statements plus unverified results.
