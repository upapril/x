## 4. Tensions, contradictions, and uncertainty

- **“Content will be mostly US stocks” vs. continued crypto/culture posts.** The 12 Oct 2026 pin announces a US-stock center of gravity; November still includes crypto-adjacent and esports stories. **Interpretation:** a shift of *emphasis*, not a hard break. The bio still advertises OKX.
- **Bubble language vs. “buy the dip / the AI game lasts another year.”** The same pinned post says “历史级别泡沫” and also that a midterm low is an entry for next year’s returns. **Interpretation:** he is not arguing “avoid AI entirely,” but “wait for a politically driven dip inside a still-running AI cycle.” That is a tension in tone, not necessarily a contradiction.
- **Method range.** 2024 “测运” sits beside 2026 Polymarket/Decision Desk tables and earnings-capex checklists. **Fact:** both exist in the retrieved sample. **Interpretation:** the account’s style is eclectic; 2024 is too thinly sampled to say which mode dominated that year.
- **Self-reported money.** Bio “千万美金美股实盘”; pin “本金300多万美金 … 三个月最高赚了360万美金ibkr账号”; “还有一些其他管理的账号，那就不展示了.” Unverified. Not used here as fact.
- **Severe coverage bias.** ~1.8% of the reported 19,982 posts. Almost all of 2022–2025 in this pass is late December (or one June 2024 week). Evolution claims should be treated as **hypotheses grounded in the available slices**, to be tested when more months are filled.
- **Short year-end note.** The 31 Dec 2022 post’s stored `text` ends at “起点的热门小说我基本都看完了” and has no `note_tweet`. It reads like the opening of a longer reflection. The archive has no further text for that id.
- **Legal identity** is not inferred. The Polymarket Traders badge is an affiliation shown by the platform, not a formal employment verification by this research.
