## Core operating model

**Interpretation (not a title he gave himself):** in the later, better-sampled 2026 posts, the operating idea is a **plan-first, anti-overconfidence, average-into-the-plan trader-influencer**. He publishes dated session plans (Nasdaq, SOXL, TQQQ), tells readers not to stare at the screen, and repeats a motto that over-ambition (“格局”) is how you get harvested. When the plan is “buy the dip,” he reports buying more as the price falls. When the plan is “don’t force a view,” he reports staying flat or taking a small short and throwing it away.

The motto, as he wrote it on 25 Mar 2026:

> 其他继续空仓等机会。还是那句：不格局就不被割，每天提醒自己一句。

Source: [2036625322649768054](https://x.com/iiiinvest/status/2036625322649768054) (2026-03-25 10:04:50 GMT+8). Repeated five days later as “别格局就不被割（每天提醒自己一遍）”: [2038432763276320796](https://x.com/iiiinvest/status/2038432763276320796) (2026-03-30 09:46:57 GMT+8).

This is a very different texture from @silverfang88’s “only play the next new story.” Here the emphasis is **not over-committing to a grand posture**, plus **execute a written plan**. It is also, unmistakably, a public teaching-and-community business: a pinned “免费” group that charges a refundable ticket and requires a Binance referral, wrapped around self-reported multi-million results and a “慈善基金.”

**What this model is not.** It is not a verified edge. “越跌越买” without a published hard invalidation is averaging, not a complete risk system. Share counts and dollar figures in 2026 plans are **his reported fills**, not risk percentages this research will generalize or recommend. Early 2022 posts in this store are mostly short casual replies, so the polished 2026 planner-guru voice is a later public persona, not something we can show as a continuous formal system from account creation.

## Explicit rules, interpretation, and a practical test

### 1. 不格局就不被割

**Explicit.** See the 25 and 30 Mar 2026 posts above. In context, 25 Mar: Trump-driven bounces are weakening; he may short Nasdaq briefly and “择机就扔”; gold looks like a bear he might short at the next high; otherwise **stay cash and wait**. 30 Mar: after a sharp drop, “别有执念,” opportunities appear, principal style is still “高空为主,” but “不格局赚钱就扔”; a weekend gold short was already closed; a 零跑 position is held.

| Layer | Content |
|---|---|
| **Explicit** | A daily reminder: if you don’t “格局,” you don’t get harvested. In the same posts he allows small, disposable shorts, cash, and taking profits quickly rather than forcing a large conviction posture. |
| **Interpretation** | “格局” here is closer to **ego-sizing / needing the market to vindicate a big stance** than to “having a framework.” The operational content is: wait, don’t marry the trade, be willing to throw it away. That is psychologically useful and still incomplete as risk management — it does not specify how large a loss ends the idea. |
| **Practical test (ours, not his rule)** | Before a week starts, write whether you are in “wait / cash,” “small disposable tactical trade,” or “held position,” and what would make you *exit* rather than endure. If your language is only “不格局,” you have a slogan, not a plan. Do not convert his motto into a made-up percent cap. |

## Decision frameworks, grounded in posts

### Signal vs noise

He tells readers that anxious tape-watching is the problem and that a plan plus “关键时间看一眼” is enough (24 Nov 2026, above). In March 2026 he treats weakening “TACO / 川普拉盘” as a fading signal and prefers waiting or small disposable shorts rather than a big directional “格局.”

**Interpretation.** Noise is emotional attachment and continuous watching; signal is whatever made it into the plan. The weakness is that we rarely see him specify the *source* quality of the plan (primary data vs headline vs intuition).

**Practical test (ours).** Write the plan the night before, then spend the session only at pre-chosen times. Afterward, mark which plan inputs were headlines vs levels vs inventory. This tests *your* process, not his edge.

### Thesis vs timing

30 Mar 2026 he reminds readers he had already said the year’s US stock market would not be a trend year, and therefore “不格局.” That is a **regime thesis** (range / no trend) used to justify tactical, short-hold trades rather than a year-long long. 24 Nov 2026 is the opposite texture: a **session thesis** (buy semiconductor / Nasdaq beta on a headline-driven air pocket) executed immediately with adds.

**Practical test (ours).** Label each idea “regime” or “session.” If you use a no-trend-year thesis, selling strength and not averaging a 3x/2x product lower is more consistent with that thesis than the November plan is. The point is consistency, not that either day was “right.”

### Entries, exits, stops, invalidation

Entries in the cited 2026 posts are often priced and dated (45 / 41 SOXL, 48 TQQQ, then 39 for another 5,000 SOXL). Exits appear as “择机就扔,” “赚钱就扔,” and “已经平掉了” on a gold short — **attitude and after-the-fact closes**, not a standing stop rule. “爆仓” appears in the group benefits as an event he will partially rebate fees for, which is an admission that blowups happen among followers, not a published personal stop algorithm.

This research found motto-level risk language and some trade management, **not** a general stop-loss percentage or invalidation template that can be quoted as his standing rule. **None will be invented.**

**Practical test (ours).** Add the missing line: “If price / thesis X happens, the plan is done.” Average-down plans that lack that line are unfinished.

### Position sizing and leverage

He reports large notional figures (a position “总仓位300万美金不到”; 20,000-share clips; a November account story of 40万→400万). These are **self-reported inventory and performance**, not a sizing *method* (no “risk 1%,” no leverage cap). SOXL and TQQQ are leveraged products; he does not, in the cited posts, explain a leverage budget. Do not infer one.

**Practical test (ours).** If you study these posts, convert *your* intended risk to a max loss you can survive if the add-on-dips plan fails immediately. That number should be yours, not attributed to him.

### Discipline, journaling, review

The public form of discipline is the **dated plan post** and the daily motto reminder, plus occasional mood posts (22 Nov 2026, [2124304416857891005](https://x.com/iiiinvest/status/2124304416857891005): “这两天有点Emo… 我想陪你走完这段时间”). That last item is community accompaniment, not a review of fills versus plan. We do not have a consistent public journal of missed plans or losing weeks comparable to @silverfang88’s “满仓挨打” confession.

**Practical test (ours).** After each week, score only: plan written? plan followed? invalidation written? result is secondary in the exercise.

## Contradictions, failure modes, monetization, survivorship

1. **“免费” vs ticket + mandatory Binance referral.** He addresses the tension by calling the ticket refundable and pointing to charity and anti-spam. A reader can accept that explanation and still note the commercial structure.
2. **Inconsistent self-reported performance and rank, all unverified.** Examples in this store:
   - Pin, 13 Oct 2026: “从10万到5000多万” — [2110022220131226106](https://x.com/iiiinvest/status/2110022220131226106).
   - 13 Nov 2026: a Binance live account “年初40万美金，到现在盈利400万美金，收益率800%,” “盈利排名又进入前3,” “前两名收益率都没我高” — [2121172719681294532](https://x.com/iiiinvest/status/2121172719681294532) (2026-11-13 17:26:00 GMT+8).
   These can describe **different accounts, different start dates, or different scopes**. They can also be marketing. This research cannot tell. They are **not** additive proof of a 50x career plus an 800% year, and they are not a backtest.
3. **不格局 vs large, aggressive reported inventory.** “Small” is relative to a self-claimed multi-million book. 20,000-share adds in leveraged ETFs are not, by themselves, a conservative process. The motto and the fill size can coexist only if “不格局” means “willing to exit,” not “small risk.” He does not always say which.
4. **越跌越买 vs missing published invalidation.** The main technical failure mode for a follower is to copy the add and not have a kill switch.
5. **Survivorship / leaderboard theater.** “又进入前3” is a snapshot of a named account. Leaderboards do not show the accounts that did not survive, or the ones not displayed.
6. **Monetization.** Referral commissions, a ticketed group, public fills, and “I will accompany you through this week” are an audience business. Educational value and distribution incentive are entangled.
7. **Coverage.** A doctrine built only from 2026 samples will overfit the posts that happened to be retrieved.

## What a careful reader can actually take

The transferable *study* object is: **write a plan, wait when you do not have one, do not need the market to crown a big persona, check the tape only at decision points — and then add the invalidation line he often skips.** Do not take “免费,” self-reported ranks, or a 10万到5000多万 story as evidence. Do not copy share counts or average-down recipes as if they were a tested system.
