## 3. Recurring main arguments

1. **Trade a written plan; do not improvise from anxiety.** “制定好卖出计划，按时执行”; “按照计划越跌越买”; “别焦虑，别害怕.”
2. **Waiting / not forcing a position is a skill.** “不格局就不被割”; “善战者无赫赫之功，等待是一项重要能力”; cash is allowed.
3. **Distinguish crash pain from grind-down pain; don’t catch knives in a funding/rate grind.** 13 Nov 2026 “阴跌” / “不要接飞刀” post.
4. **Short-term lows can be “情绪+机械” selling and are hard to break immediately** — a claim he repeats around the 5 Nov 2026 Nasdaq low (15 Nov review).
5. **Community against fraud, plus a self-described charity/referral structure.** Pinned 13 Oct 2026 post; bio already pushes a Binance referral and “电鱼哥实盘.”
6. **Self-presentation as a successful live trader who “has earned enough.”** Bio (10万到6000万, 排名第二, 冲亿); pinned (10万到5000多万); 13 Nov 2026 (one Binance account 40万→400万, top-3). These are claims, not audited results.

## 4. Tensions, contradictions, and uncertainty

- **“免费” group vs. paid ticket + referral.** The pinned post is titled/opens “建群了（免费）” but requires $9 USDT and an exclusive Binance signup whose rebates go to the “基金.” **Fact:** both statements are in the same post. **Interpretation:** “免费” refers to not charging a membership fee, while other payments are framed as refundable tickets and charity. Readers can reasonably see tension between the anti-“割韭菜” rhetoric and the referral/ticket design. This analysis does not assert fraud; it records the structure as posted.
- **Inconsistent self-reported numbers.** Bio: 10万到**6000万**, 排名**第二**. Pinned (13 Oct 2026): 10万到**5000多万**. 13 Nov 2026: a named Binance account at **800%** and “又进入前3,” with “前两名收益率都没我高.” 15 Nov: “赚5000万也堵不上一张臭嘴.” **Interpretation:** figures may refer to different accounts, dates, or rhetoric; they are not mutually reconciled in the posts themselves and are unverified.
- **Style vs. early feed.** 2022 casual jokes and World Cup bets versus 2026 highly formatted daily plans. That is evolution of the public persona, not a logical contradiction.
- **Sampling.** 2023–2024 arguments are under-determined. Many November 2026 posts are short replies (“小群”) that add little to worldview. Trading recommendations in the archive are **not** investment advice and were not back-tested here.
- **Legal identity** is unknown and is not inferred.
