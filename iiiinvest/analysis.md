## Practical framework to study (ours)

**Explicit in his posts:** write a plan; interpret relative strength/volume; scale in or out conditionally; invalidate an idea at a stated threshold in some examples; hold when the plan calls for it; “越跌越买/越跌越补” as planned dip-buying.

**Not supplied universally:** account loss limit, aggregated leverage cap, treatment of gaps and liquidation, or a fixed rule choosing “hold” versus “不格局”.

**Study recommendations:** select a public plan with a timestamp before its trigger; record the original threshold without hindsight changes; find the next related update; classify “triggered/reported execution/no follow-up.” Track losing and winning cases equally. For a grid overlay, require equity, notional, benchmark return, funding, fees and drawdown over the same horizon. This corpus offers case studies, not a statistically complete strategy sample. Do not invent a percent-of-equity risk cap and call it his.

Keep the disagreement with SEPA’s documented “never average down” iron rule **explicit**: @iiiinvest’s retrieved posts repeatedly plan adding as price falls. The two sources do not settle which rule is better. See `FINANCE_COMPARISON.md` and `FINANCE_SKILLS_COMPARISON.md`.

## Contradictions and incentives

- **Take profits / do not marry a view vs “拿住”:** possibly regime-dependent, but no complete regime-switch policy is established. Do not flatten them into one timeless rule or declare inconsistency where the horizon differs.
- **Stop discipline vs aggressive capital-tier advice:** a correct stop example does not neutralize ruin risk from leveraged all-in sizing.
- **Independent process vs “等我信号”:** followers may not share the author's trigger, latency or exit.
- **“免费” community vs refundable ticket/referral:** [2026-10-13 22:57:54 GMT+8](https://x.com/iiiinvest/status/2110022220131226106) describes the gate and fund claims. Intent and financial transparency are not independently audited; career-return and fund guarantees are self-report.
