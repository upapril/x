## Rules versus a researcher's how-to

**Stated (explicit):** focus on arb; admit only a few directional exceptions; keep personal risk leverage low enough to execute (with dated 0.5–2 and later 0.25x language that are not the same object); inspect protocol documentation and redemption mechanics; abandon opportunities whose profit/risk/capacity does not fit. The low-risk pool return/drawdown summary in [2025-11-14 10:51:32 GMT+8](https://x.com/taresky/status/1989164267254464681) is an author self-report, not capital-verified P&L.

**Study recommendations, not his advertised system:** map every leg, collateral currency, borrow cost, funding path, redemption permission and latency; model a depeg/withdrawal freeze plus borrow-rate spike; record gross leverage separately from net delta and liquidation distance; calculate capacity after fees. Test the operational route in simulation, not by copying historical yield figures. The corpus does not supply full code, positions, hedge ratios or audited cash flows.
