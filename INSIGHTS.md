It is not a ranking of who is “the better trader,” and it is not proof that anyone has an edge. None of the performance numbers quoted in their posts were independently verified. No backtest was run. Read the per-account `analysis.md` files for the dated URLs and the explicit-vs-interpretation-vs-test breakdown. Coverage is incomplete in every folder — especially @silverfang88 in 2025 (only 25 stored posts) and much of 2024/2026, @iiiinvest after early 2024, and @alexandr_wang in 2012–14 plus most of 2017–2023. ## 2. Similarities (what actually repeats)

1. **Stories, not isolated ticks.** @silverfang88 talks in narratives (NFT, Friend.tech, memecoins, midterm/AI). @iiiinvest talks in dated “plans” attached to a regime story (no-trend year; headline-driven air pocket). Wang talks in industry and company stories (Scale’s data bottleneck, US–China AI race, Muse, safety). The shared skill being *claimed* is the ability to say what story you are in.
2. **Public confidence is part of the product.** All three post with a decisive voice. That is not the same thing as a checked fact. Wang’s 26 Dec 2022 COVID line ([1607219151059423234](https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/1607219151059423234)) and the traders’ P&L posts belong in the same *reader* habit: separate tone from evidence.
3. **The two trading accounts monetize attention.** Groups, referrals, calls, pins, “I’ll walk this week with you,” exchange links. @silverfang88’s Friend.tech call post ([1702448718279241959](https://x.com/silverfang88/status/1702448718279241959)) and @iiiinvest’s “建群了（免费）” pin ([2110022220131226106](https://x.com/iiiinvest/status/2110022220131226106)) are not side issues; they are the distribution layer around the advice.
4. **Self-reported performance is a pin-level object, not an audit.** @silverfang88: IBKR “本金300多万美金 / 三个月最高赚了360万美金,” other accounts “那就不展示了” ([2109440726865985560](https://x.com/silverfang88/status/2109440726865985560)). @iiiinvest: “10万到5000多万” in the pin, and separately a Binance account “40万→盈利400万 / 800% / 前3” ([2121172719681294532](https://x.com/iiiinvest/status/2121172719681294532)). These are claims, possibly about different books, with withheld accounts and leaderboard snapshots. **They are not additive, not verified, and not a study result.**
5. **Process slogans are more common than published invalidation.** Both traders say things a careful trader would want (plan, wait, don’t marry a bag, take profit, sometimes cut). Hard, reusable “if X, I am wrong, I exit” language is scarce relative to motto and fill commentary.

## 4. Failure modes (for a reader, not a morality play)

1. **Survivorship and the withheld book.** “其他管理的账号 / 那就不展示了” and “又进入前3” are structurally the same problem: you are looking at what can be shown today.
2. **Leaderboard and pin inflation.** Different accounts, different starting capital, different time windows, and screenshots can be lined up to look like one career. They should be kept as separate, unverified claims.
3. **Monetization distortion.** Calls, referrals, ticketed “free” groups, and bots will bias the feed toward frequency, confidence, and new stories. That can coincide with real research; it can also be a distribution strategy.
4. **Slogan without invalidation.** “不格局,” “只玩下一个新的故事,” “按照计划,” “卖飞光荣” all fail open if you never write the condition that ends the trade.
5. **Copying dated recipes out of context.** The 6 Aug 2023 memecoin ladder is not a 2026 US-stock policy. The 24 Nov 2026 SOXL adds are not a 2023 on-chain playbook. Neither is a backtested rule.
6. **Average-down plus leveraged beta.** @iiiinvest’s explicit “越跌越买” on SOXL/TQQQ is a specific, high-velocity tactic. Copying it without a kill switch is the obvious mechanical failure.
7. **梭 into a bubble you already named.** @silverfang88’s pin holds both. The failure mode is that “timing the scare” feels like risk management and is still a concentrated directional bet.
8. **Coverage bias.** A 2025 theory about @silverfang88 built on 25 posts is almost certainly wrong as history.
9. **Category error on Wang.** Treating a CAIO’s authenticity or safety posts as a trading system is inventing a guru. The only honest use is analogy, then your own test.
10. **Confusing confession with a fix.** “我忏悔 我认错” is evidence the prior rule was broken, not evidence the next trade will follow the rule.

## 5. A practical, evidence-grounded trader study checklist

This checklist is **ours**. It is how to *study* these feeds (and any similar ones) without pretending their P&L is a track record. It is not their rules, and it is not a strategy offer.

**A. Before you treat a post as a “lesson”**

1. Save the URL, the Asia/Shanghai date, and the market it was about (NFT, memecoin, ARB, midterms, SOXL…).
2. Split the text into **explicit wording**, **your interpretation**, and **a test you could run**. If you cannot fill all three, you only have a vibe.
3. Ask whether the account was also selling a group, a referral, a call, or a pin that day. Note it; don’t pretend it is irrelevant.
4. Search the corpus for a later contradiction (the working pair in this package is Aug 2023 rules vs Mar 2024 满仓挨打; “免费” vs 9 USDT + Binance link).

**B. The decision questions the posts themselves raise — and the line they often skip**

5. **Signal vs noise.** What, in the post, is a primary source versus a recycled take or a headline? @silverfang88’s 2022 Spaces exit and 2023 “信息搜集能力至上” are useful prompts. Your test: log a week of inputs by source type.
6. **Thesis vs timing.** Write two sentences, not one. @silverfang88’s 2026 pin is the teaching example (AI game ≥1 year *and* wait for midterm fear). Wang’s *analogy*: “the path is not inevitable” plus delay. Your test: if you cannot wait while keeping the long thesis, you have collapsed the two.
7. **Entry.** Is this buy-strength, buy-dip, or wait-for-scare? Do not average the three men (or even two years of one man) into a single entry rule.
8. **Exit and invalidation.** Quote only exits they actually wrote. Then add the line most of these posts lack: “If X happens, the idea is wrong and I exit.” Your test fails if that line is missing.
9. **Sizing and leverage.** Use only numbers they posted, labeled as *their dated claim* (a 2023 scale-out, a 2026 reported fill). **Do not invent a percent risk, a leverage cap, or a “they always risk Y” rule.** Your test: define *your* max loss before following any 梭 or 越跌越买 thought.
10. **Discipline.** Prefer journals that compare plan vs fill vs emotion. @iiiinvest’s “write a plan, don’t stare” is the prompt; @silverfang88’s confession is the audit example. Your test: one week scored on “plan written / plan followed / invalidation written,” with P&L as a footnote, not the grade.

**C. Performance and marketing hygiene (non-negotiable)**

11. Record every self-reported P&L or rank as **claim**, with date and URL, and never sum claims across posts or accounts.
12. If other accounts or time windows are withheld, write “selection filter present.”
13. Do not use phrases like “he proved,” “backed by returns,” or “the edge is.” This package cannot support them.
14. If you change your own process after reading them, write down the change *and* a date to review it. If you cannot, you took a slogan, not a lesson.

**D. Using Wang at all**

15. Only after labeling the idea as **analogy**. Candidate analogies already grounded in his posts: don’t please the timeline (authenticity reply); don’t treat a path as fate that forces immediate risk (23 Nov 2026); name a failure *mode* and fix that, rather than “guessing” (SEAL / failure-mode post, [1907604573730288060](https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/1907604573730288060)). The Muse delay, “SI Accords,” and RSI-compute lines in this package are Wang’s 23 Nov 2026 post ([2124719968499777546](https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/2124719968499777546)). Packaging notes cite a September 2026 Reuters URL for a Zuckerberg remark on the delay; this integration could not re-read that page (HTTP 401), so it is not treated as reconfirmed wire text.

## 6. One compact study plan (two weeks, no copied size)

If the goal is “how to do things, how to be a good trader” in the sense of *using this material well*:

- **Week 1 — read, don’t trade their ideas.** Pick five dated posts (at least one contradiction pair). Fill the three-layer table for each. Write your own invalidation line where they omitted it.
- **Week 2 — process only.** Trade *your* normal instrument, or sit on your hands, but run the checklist: plan before the session, source log, thesis/timing split, pre-committed max loss *you* chose, end-of-week audit against the plan. Do not import their share counts, 梭, or 1x/3x/5x ladder as defaults.

The insight worth keeping is not which persona to imitate. It is that **useful trading thought in these feeds is sparse, dated, and often contradicted by later posts**, and that the work of a serious reader is to extract the decision distinction (next story vs last bag; plan vs tape; thesis vs timing; rule vs later behavior) and then **hold yourself to the missing invalidation and the unverifiable P&L warning** — the parts the accounts cannot do for you.
